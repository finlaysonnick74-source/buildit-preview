About

Hi, I’m Tomas. This is my story, short and honest.

I’m 15 and I live on Sydney’s Northern Beaches. BuildIT is my web studio, and it’s how I’m turning my life around.

Where I was

Not long ago I fell in with the wrong crowd. I made bad choices and did things I’m not proud of. I hurt people, including my family. I’m saying that straight. I’m not asking anyone to feel sorry for me.

What changed

I chose to change. I want focus, a work ethic and something I can be proud of. BuildIT gives me all three. Every site I build is a promise I keep, and every promise kept is a bit of trust earned back.

I’ve also learned a lot about myself. I have ADHD, and I’m learning how my brain works and how to work with it. I practise meditation, I’m working on stopping to think before I act, and I’m getting better at being on time.

Why it’s public

Doing this in public keeps me honest. You can see the work, follow my progress and check every site yourself. If I get something wrong, I own it and I fix it.

My values

Six things I’m building my life on.

  • TrustI earn it back one kept promise at a time.
  • IntegrityI do the right thing, even when no one is checking.
  • Work ethicI turn up, put the hours in and finish what I start.
  • Giving backI put something good back into the community I took from.
  • TeamworkI listen to people with more experience and learn from them.
  • Owning itWhat I did is mine to own, and so is fixing it.

Giving back

“To give back to the community for what I have taken.”

Tomas

My first 5 websites are free, in exchange for an honest review. Charity and community sites are always free, separate from those five.

Who you’ll be talking to

Me, directly. I answer the phone, I reply to the emails and I build the sites. Call or text 0403 993 241 or email tomas@buildit.net.au.

Why trust me?

“I’m publicly showing the world my transformation, my journey from troubled to focused. Because of this, everything I do is in the public eye. So I’m determined to learn the skills and apply them, along with my work ethic, so my websites are the best possible outcomes for customers, and at the same time transform my life.”

Tomas
