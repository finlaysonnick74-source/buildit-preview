    A site for WaldeComms, a Sydney PR agency that turns businesses into brands people can’t ignore. The owner asked for changes, and I made them before it went on here.

    What it taught me: WaldeComms taught me to listen to feedback and keep working until the client is happy.

    See the site ↗
  • Step 1 of five

    Khemistry

    A site for Khemistry Pty Ltd, a recruitment business built around one idea: people, place and purpose. It has a periodic table intro I built from scratch. This is the one I’m most proud of.

    What it taught me: Khemistry taught me to start with the client’s story, then build the design around it.

    See the site ↗

  • Perfect Jobs & Adventures

    A full site for a Sydney business helping 18 to 35 year olds settle in and see Australia: jobs, adventures and arrival packages.

    What it taught me: This one taught me to plan every page before I write any code.

  • Perfect Au Pairs

    An au pair agency site with matching for families and au pairs, plus a big Harbour Bridge hero.

    What it taught me: This one taught me to keep a big site simple to use.

    Visit live site ↗

  • Lux-Lashes

    A site for a mobile lash artist on the Northern Beaches, with private appointments at home.

    What it taught me: This one taught me that small details matter on a small site.
