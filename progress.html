Website by website

What I built, what was hard, what I learned, and a link so you can check it yourself.

  1. Site 01

    Perfect Jobs & Adventures my proudest

    What I built
    A full site for a Sydney business helping 18 to 35 year olds settle in and see Australia: jobs, adventures and arrival packages.
    What was hard
    Fitting a lot of information onto every page without it feeling crowded.
    What I learned
    Plan the content first, then design. Cutting words makes a page better.
    Visit live site ↗
  2. Site 02

    Perfect Au Pairs

    What I built
    An au pair agency site with matching for families and au pairs, plus a big Harbour Bridge hero.
    What was hard
    Making the matching pages clear for two different groups of people.
    What I learned
    Think about who is reading each page, and write for them.
    Visit live site ↗
  3. Site 03

    Lux-Lashes

    What I built
    A site for a mobile lash artist on the Northern Beaches, with private appointments at home.
    What was hard
    Getting the look right for the client, not just what I liked.
    What I learned
    Listen properly, then show work early and often.
    Visit live site ↗
  4. Site 04

    Innovo

    What I built
    A site for a technology company that does enterprise AI and automation.
    What was hard
    Explaining technical services in plain English.
    What I learned
    If I can’t explain it simply, I don’t understand it yet.
    Visit live site ↗
