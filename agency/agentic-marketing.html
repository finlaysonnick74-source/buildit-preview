Questions

Straight answers.

What does "agentic" mean?

Agentic means able to act on its own. Our AI agents don't just answer prompts: they plan, make decisions and complete multi-step work toward the goals you set, from story pitches and posts to research, emails, follow-ups and booking. I set the plan, check the work and look after you.

Can I take just PR, just marketing or just sales?

Yes. Take all three, or just the one you need, and add the others later. See the three modules.

Is it just AI spam?

No. Every post and email is written for your business and for the person reading it. Emails go out in small batches, from your own address, and I check the work.

Is the email outreach legal?

Yes. Emails go to businesses where your offer is relevant, say who you are, and include a clear way to unsubscribe, as Australia's Spam Act requires. Anyone who opts out is never contacted again.

Do you guarantee meetings or sales?

No one honest can. I promise the work: daily content, steady prospecting, fast follow-up and a report with real numbers.

Do I approve what goes out?

You set the dial: approve everything, approve some, or let it run. You always approve the target customer and the 30-day plan. You can ask to see posts and emails before they go out, and you can pause or change anything at any time.

Who built the technology?

The agentic engine is Innovo IP, built by Innovo Technology Solutions. BuildIT licenses it and runs it for businesses across Australia.

Which CRM do you use?

HubSpot, or the CRM you already have. Every prospect, reply and meeting is logged there, and it's yours to keep.

What do I need to give you?

Your offer, your ideal customer, access to your social accounts and calendar, and photos or clips of your work when you have them. I'll show you how to share access safely. You never send me a password in a message.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. It's a monthly retainer with 30 days' notice. The set-up fee covers the strategy, profiles, CRM and sending set-up.

Who owns the accounts, contacts and content?

You do. Your accounts, your followers, your CRM contacts, your posts. If you leave, everything stays with you.

Do I need a website from you?

No, any package works on its own. But a good website is where interest turns into enquiries, so the Launch Bundle gives you both. See website packages.

How much does it cost?

Each package is a fixed monthly price with no lock-in. Talk to Tomas and he'll quote the right one for your business. Quotes are ex GST, and ad spend for paid ads is extra and paid straight to the platform.
